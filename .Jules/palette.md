## 2025-01-31 - [Arabic Localization & Accessibility]
**Learning:** In RTL/Arabic interfaces, it's critical to maintain language consistency in all ARIA labels. Even dynamic labels (like theme switchers) should be fully localized to avoid jarring context switches for screen reader users.
**Action:** Always provide localized Arabic equivalents for state-based ARIA labels (e.g., light -> فاتح, dark -> داكن).
